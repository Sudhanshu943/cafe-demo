# Inputs for the later website plan

Research has produced options; these decisions remain open. This file is a handoff checklist, not an implementation plan.

## Business and brand

- Café name, logo and positioning: neighborhood hangout, specialty coffee studio, bakery café or another direction.
- Real audience, city, location count and venue timezone.
- Primary action: visit, order ahead, reserve or buy packaged coffee.
- Existing brand colors, photography and content rights.
- Confirmed menu, prices, dietary information, hours, contact and access details.

## Design selection

- Choose among warm editorial, botanical modern and midnight studio.
- Choose hero format: static 3D render, layered image or real-time cup.
- Decide whether a short brewing scroll story adds enough value.
- Decide whether gallery, events, reviews, newsletter, shop or reservations are operationally justified.
- Agree how expressive motion should be, and which devices must receive the full effect.

## Validation before committing to a complex build

- Visually inspect the shortlisted sites on desktop and mobile; capture only reference material we can legitimately retain.
- Compare a static hero and an interactive cup prototype on a representative midrange phone.
- Confirm that a visitor can find prices, hours and directions quickly with keyboard and touch.
- Test chosen palette pairs, font readability, zoom and reduced-motion behavior.
- Check the selected libraries' exact versions, interoperability, license terms and runtime transfer costs.
- Acquire a small final asset set with individual provenance records.

## Recommended starting hypothesis

Warm editorial direction; clear menu and visit actions; genuine venue photography; one reusable cup asset; native scrolling; selective UI transitions; progressive 3D enhancement with a matching poster. Validate this hypothesis before turning it into a build plan.
