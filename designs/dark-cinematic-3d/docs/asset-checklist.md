# Dark Cinematic 3D — asset checklist

Status: requirements only; nothing acquired by this plan.

- **Hero GLB:** Complete cup and saucer, clean normals, controlled camera limits and compact textures. Include source and provenance.
- **Matching poster:** Desktop/mobile stills from the same camera and lighting as the live scene.
- **Environment:** A licensed compact lighting environment or baked lighting; no unnecessary large HDRI transfer.
- **Venue photographs:** Real room and exterior images for trust and grounding.
